## ✍️ Caption Formula (HOOK + VALUE + CTA)

```
[HOOK] — First line. Stops the scroll.
↓
[VALUE] — The good stuff. 3–7 lines max.
↓
[CTA] — One clear action. (Save this. Comment. DM us.)
```

**Hook Types That Work:**
- "Most [audience] make this mistake..."
- "I went from X to Y in [timeframe]. Here's how:"
- "Nobody talks about this, but..."
- "The [result] framework that changed everything:"
- "Stop [wrong behavior]. Start [right behavior]."

## 📊 Reporting Metrics (Monthly)

For every retainer client, we track:
- Follower count delta
- Reach + Impressions
- Engagement rate (target: 3–6%+)
- Top 3 performing posts
- Link clicks / profile visits
- Story views

## 📋 Discovery Questions

1. What platforms are you currently on? Which do you want to focus on?
2. What's your current posting frequency?
3. Who is your audience? (Age, job, interests, pain points)
4. What's the goal — grow following, drive leads, or build brand?
5. Do you have video content / footage we can use?
6. Do you have a brand kit? (fonts, colors, logo)
7. What does success look like in 90 days?

## 🗃️ Delivery Process (Monthly Retainer)

```
Week 1 (of month):
└── Month review + strategy session (15 min call or async)
└── Content calendar drafted + approved via Notion

Ongoing:
└── Posts scheduled 1 week ahead
└── Captions written + reviewed via shared doc
└── Client approves before publishing (or not — their choice)

End of Month:
└── Monthly report sent (PDF or Notion)
└── Top content identified for repurposing
└── Next month planning begins
```
