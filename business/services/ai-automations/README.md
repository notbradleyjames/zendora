## 🛠️ Our Tech Stack (The 4 Pillars)

Following the systems mindset, we structure our stack across four key components:

### 1. AI Models (The "Brain")
- **OpenAI GPT-4o** — Default LLM
- **Anthropic Claude** — For nuanced writing/reasoning tasks
- **Groq (Llama 3)** — For fast, cost-efficient processing

### 2. Orchestration & Connectors (The "Nerves")
- **n8n** — Primary automation builder (self-hosted or cloud)
- **Make.com** — For clients already on Make
- **Zapier** — Simple consumer-grade automations only

### 3. Databases (The "Memory")
- Airtable, Google Sheets, Supabase, Notion CRM

### 4. Connective Tissue & Front-End (The "Interface")
- **CRMs/Comms:** HubSpot, GoHighLevel, Slack, Twilio
- **Front-Ends:** Internal dashboards, custom portals, Chat-interfaces

## 📋 Discovery Questions (Ask Every Client)

1. Walk me through a typical day — what do you do manually that you hate?
2. What tools are you currently using? (CRM, email, calendar, forms, etc.)
3. If one thing could just "happen automatically," what would it be?
4. What does a successful outcome look like in 30 days?
5. Do you have a developer on your team, or is this all managed by you?
6. What's your current monthly tool budget?

## 🗃️ Delivery Process (The ACE Framework)

We utilize the **ACE Framework** to build scalable, reliable AI Systems.

```
1. [A] Architect (Discovery & Design)
   └── Discovery Call: Identify bottlenecks; avoid automating broken processes.
   └── System Scope: Define the Models, Connectors, DBs, and Interface.
   └── Blueprint: Map out data flow (inputs to outputs) on a whiteboard/Lucidchart.

2. [C] Code (Build & Connect)
   └── V1 (The Magic Moment): Build the simplest functional version to deliver immediate ROI.
   └── Assembly: Connect the No-Code tools (n8n/Make) to the AI Models and Database.
   └── Sandbox Testing: Run mock data through all error paths in an isolated sandbox.

3. [E] Execute (Run & Refine)
   └── Live Testing: Run end-to-end with real user data.
   └── Handoff & Walkthrough: Deliver screen recording, docs, and the SOP manual.
   └── Phase 2 / Maintenance: Monitor loops, optimize prompts, offer maintenance retainer.
```
