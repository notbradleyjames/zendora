# 🔄 Client Onboarding SOP

> *Follow this every single time. No exceptions. Consistency = professionalism.*

---

## Trigger
This SOP activates the moment a client says **"yes"** — verbally, via message, or by paying a deposit.

---

## Step-by-Step Process

### ✅ Step 1 — Send the Welcome Message (Within 2 hours of "yes")

Platform-specific:

**Email:**
```
Subject: Welcome to Zendora 🎉 — Here's what happens next

Hi [NAME],

Really excited to be working with you on this!

Here's a quick overview of how we'll get started:

1. I'll send over a brief project summary to make sure we're aligned
2. [If applicable] Deposit invoice is attached — please process at your convenience
3. I'll send a short questionnaire to gather everything I need
4. We'll kick off within [X] days of receiving the above

If you have any questions before then, just reply here.

Looking forward to it,
[Your name]
Zendora
```

---

### ✅ Step 2 — Create Client Folder (Same day)

1. Duplicate `business/clients/_template/` folder
2. Rename to `business/clients/[client-name-project]/`
3. Fill in `brief.md` with all discovery call notes
4. Create `project-log.md` — log first entry: "Project kicked off"

---

### ✅ Step 3 — Send Onboarding Questionnaire (Within 24 hours)

For each service type, use the relevant form/questionnaire:
- **Design**: `hermes_core/skills/content-frameworks/README.md` → Brand Questionnaire
- **Automation**: `business/services/ai-automations/README.md` → Discovery Questions
- **Marketing**: `business/services/marketing/README.md` → Discovery Questions
- **Social Media**: `business/services/social-media/README.md` → Discovery Questions

Use Tally.so or Google Forms. Keep it under 15 questions.

---

### ✅ Step 4 — Send Invoice for Deposit (Within 24 hours)

- 50% upfront for projects over $300
- 100% upfront for projects under $300 or Fiverr orders
- Tools: Wave, PayPal, Stripe, or platform payment

---

### ✅ Step 5 — Kick-off Confirmation (Once deposit received)

Send:
```
Hi [NAME],

Deposit received — we're officially underway! 🚀

Your expected delivery date is: [DATE]
I'll have a first draft / update ready by: [EARLIER DATE]

I'll be in touch if I have any questions. Otherwise, expect to hear from me on [DATE].

[Your name]
```

---

### ✅ Step 6 — Begin Work

Checklist before starting:
- [ ] Brief is complete and clear
- [ ] All assets/access received (logins, files, brand kit, etc.)
- [ ] Deadline is in your calendar with a reminder 3 days before
- [ ] Project log is open and active

---

### ✅ Step 7 — Mid-Project Check-in (For projects >5 days)

Send a short update at the halfway point:

```
Hi [NAME], quick update — project is going well!

So far I've completed: [WHAT'S DONE]
Currently working on: [CURRENT STEP]
Still on track for: [DELIVERY DATE]

Any questions or changes before I continue? Just let me know.
```

---

### ✅ Step 8 — Delivery

1. Send files via agreed method (email, Google Drive, Dropbox, platform)
2. Include a short Loom walkthrough video (even 2–3 minutes = huge value)
3. Send final invoice for remaining balance (if applicable)
4. Log delivery in `project-log.md`

**Delivery Message Template:**
```
Subject: [PROJECT] — Delivered! 🎉

Hi [NAME],

Your project is complete and ready! Here's what's included:

📁 [Link to folder/files]
🎥 Walkthrough video: [Loom link]

[Brief description of what was delivered]

Your invoice for the remaining balance is also attached — no rush, whenever convenient.

I really enjoyed working on this. If you're happy with the results, a quick review on [Platform] would mean a lot!

And if you ever need anything else, I'm always here.

[Your name]
Zendora
```

---

### ✅ Step 9 — Post-Delivery Follow-up (3–5 days later)

```
Hi [NAME],

Just checking in — hope everything is working great!

If you have any questions or need any tweaks, just let me know.

Also, if you've had a chance to leave a review on [Platform], I'd really appreciate it — it helps more than you know.

And if you know anyone else who might need [SERVICE], I'd love an introduction!

[Your name]
```

---

### ✅ Step 10 — Close Out

- [ ] Final payment received
- [ ] Files backed up to cloud
- [ ] Client record updated in CRM/tracking sheet
- [ ] Testimonial logged
- [ ] Project log marked complete
- [ ] Debrief noted in `project-log.md` (what went well, what to improve)

---

## ⚠️ Common Pitfalls

| Pitfall | Prevention |
|---|---|
| Scope creep | Always refer back to agreed brief. New requests = new quote. |
| Slow client response | Set a 48-hr response expectation upfront |
| Missed deadline | Build 1–2 days buffer into every quoted timeline |
| Unclear revision rounds | Specify in brief: "2 rounds of revisions included" |
| Not getting the testimonial | Ask every single time, at delivery |
