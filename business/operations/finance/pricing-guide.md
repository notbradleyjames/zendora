# 💰 Pricing Guide — Zendora

> *Know your worth. Price with confidence. Never race to the bottom.*

---

## 🧮 Pricing Philosophy

1. **Price the outcome, not the hours.** Clients don't care how long it takes — they care what they get.
2. **Always anchor high.** Present a premium option first. People buy the middle.
3. **Raise rates on every 5th 5-star review.** Never stop moving up.
4. **Platform rates < Direct rates.** Fiverr = 20% fee. Factor it in.

## 🎯 Packaging Strategy (The 3-Tier Rule)

Always offer 3 tiers. People almost always buy the middle.

**Formula:**
- **Basic** = bare minimum to get the result
- **Standard** = the "right" choice (most value, most sold)
- **Premium** = everything + white glove treatment

**Example (AI Automation):**

| | Basic | Standard | Premium |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Price** | $300 | $700 | $1,500 |
| Single automation | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| 3 automations | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Documentation | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Loom walkthrough | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ |
| 2-week support | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ |
| Priority delivery | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ |

## 🧾 Payment Terms

- **Fiverr/Upwork**: Platform handles payment. No negotiation.
- **Direct (New client)**: 50% deposit upfront, 50% on delivery.
- **Direct (Returning client)**: Net 7 (invoice due within 7 days of delivery).
- **Retainers**: Invoiced on the 1st of each month. Pause/cancel with 14 days notice.

**Accepted Methods (Direct):** Stripe, PayPal, Bank transfer, Wise (international)

---

## 🚫 Discount Policy

- **No discounts** for "I'll give you a great review."
- **Student/startup discount**: Max 20%, only for projects under $500, only once per client.
- **Returning client loyalty**: 10% off second project.
- **Bundle discount**: 15% off when booking 2+ services together.
