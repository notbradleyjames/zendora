# 🏛️ Zendora: Brand Identity

A strong brand is the foundation of a premium agency. Use these tokens for all your portfolio assets, website, and communications to ensure a cohesive and high-end feel.

---

## 🎨 Color Palette: "Galactic Dawn"

| Token | Hex/Value | Usage |
|-------|-----------|-------|
| **Zendora Deep Sea** | `#0B132B` | Main background, text contrast |
| **Zendora Midnight** | `#1C2541` | Secondary backgrounds, cards |
| **Zendora Neon Purple** | `#7209B7` | Primary Action, Accents |
| **Zendora Blue Glow** | `#4CC9F0` | Interactive elements, highlights |
| **Zendora Silver** | `#E0E1DD` | Primary Text |

---

## 🖋️ Typography: "Modern Tech"

- **Heading Font**: [Inter](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter) (Bold, 800)
- **Body Font**: [Roboto](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Roboto) (Medium, 400)
- **Accent Font**: [Space Mono](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Space+Mono) (Monospace for tech snippets)

---

## 🎭 Tone of Voice: "Expert & Accessible"

1. **Authoritative**: We know our tech. We don't use "maybe" or "perhaps."
2. **Simplified**: We explain complex AI concepts in plain business language.
3. **Outcome-Obsessed**: We lead with the "Result" (ROI, Time Saved).
4. **Human-Centric**: We emphasize that our AI is *augmented* by human expertise.

---

## 🖼️ Visual Style: "Glassmorphism & Glows"

- **UI Elements**: Use subtle transparency and background blurs.
- **Iconography**: Use sleek, minimal line icons (e.g., Lucide Icons).
- **Gradients**: Use smooth transitions from `#7209B7` to `#4CC9F0`.
