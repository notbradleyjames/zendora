# 🗺️ Zendora — 90-Day Launch Roadmap

> *Your first 90 days as Zendora. Clear. Sequenced. Achievable.*

---

## 🎯 The North Star Goal (Day 90)

> **Land 3 paying clients. Earn $2,000–$5,000. Get 5 five-star reviews.**

Everything below is reverse-engineered from that goal.

---

## 📅 Month 1 — Foundation (Days 1–30)
**Theme: Set up. Show up. Start shipping.**

### Week 1 — Setup
- [ ] Finalize Zendora brand identity (logo, colors, fonts)
- [ ] Create Fiverr account → Publish 2–3 starter gigs (use `services/freelance-gigs/README.md`)
- [ ] Optimize LinkedIn profile (headline, banner, about, featured)
- [ ] Set up Notion/Airtable as your CRM (track all leads)
- [ ] Set up Wave for invoicing (free)
- [ ] Set up Calendly for discovery call booking
- [ ] Pick your lean tool stack (`operations/tools.md` → Recommended Start Stack)

### Week 2–3 — Content Launch
- [ ] Post 3x/week on LinkedIn (use `services/social-media/linkedin-starter-pack.md`)
- [ ] Engage on 10 other posts per day (comments, not just likes)
- [ ] Apply to 5 Upwork jobs (use `/upwork-proposal` workflow)
- [ ] DM 10 people you already know → tell them what you're building
- [ ] Record 1 Loom video explaining what Zendora does → post it

### Week 4 — First Clients
- [ ] Offer 1–2 "beta" projects at 30% reduced rate in exchange for testimonials
- [ ] Complete the beta project using your SOPs (even for beta clients — practice!)
- [ ] Request review + testimonial immediately after delivery
- [ ] Refine your proposal template based on what questions came up

**Month 1 Revenue Target:** $0–$1,000 (reviews > revenue this month)

---

## 📅 Month 2 — Traction (Days 31–60)
**Theme: Refine the machine. Build volume.**

### Content & Visibility
- [ ] Maintain 3x/week LinkedIn posting (no breaks)
- [ ] Add 1 platform: Instagram or Twitter/X
- [ ] Repurpose your top-performing LinkedIn posts for the new platform
- [ ] Start collecting a portfolio — screenshot/record everything you build

### Sales
- [ ] Apply to 5+ Upwork jobs per day (you have reviews now → conversion goes up)
- [ ] Raise Fiverr prices by 15–20% after first 5 reviews
- [ ] Reach out to 5 new LinkedIn connections per day (not pitching — connecting)
- [ ] Follow up with anyone who liked/commented posts but didn't reach out

### Delivery
- [ ] Complete 2–4 projects this month
- [ ] Use every SOP religiously — build the habit now
- [ ] Log every win, every lesson in `clients/[name]/project-log.md`

**Month 2 Revenue Target:** $800–$2,000

---

## 📅 Month 3 — Scale (Days 61–90)
**Theme: Package. Price up. Repeat.**

### Revenue Optimization
- [ ] Introduce your first **retainer offer** (social media or automation maintenance)
- [ ] Pitch retainer to your best Month 1–2 clients first
- [ ] Raise direct rates by 20% minimum
- [ ] Bundle services for higher AOV (e.g., "Brand Kit + Social Templates + 30-Day Calendar")

### Systems
- [ ] Automate your own client onboarding (eat your own cooking — build it in n8n)
- [ ] Create 1 templated case study from your best project so far
- [ ] Publish the case study on LinkedIn + Fiverr profile

### Growth
- [ ] Ask every completed client for 1 referral
- [ ] Apply to 1 higher-ticket platform (Toptal, Contra, or direct outreach to agencies)
- [ ] Look at adding 1 new Fiverr gig based on what buyers are actually asking for
- [ ] Review all prices → raise anything that's under-priced

**Month 3 Revenue Target:** $1,500–$4,000

## 📊 KPIs to Track Weekly

| Metric | Week 1–4 Target | Week 5–8 Target | Week 9–12 Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn posts published | 3/wk | 3/wk | 3–4/wk |
| LinkedIn engagement (comments given) | 50/wk | 60/wk | 70/wk |
| Upwork proposals sent | 20/wk | 25/wk | 20/wk |
| Fiverr gigs live | 2–3 | 3–5 | 5–7 |
| Active projects | 0–1 | 1–3 | 2–4 |
| Revenue | $0–$500 | $500–$1,500 | $1,000–$3,000 |
| Reviews earned | 0–2 | 3–6 | 7–10 |

---

## ⚠️ The 3 Landmines to Avoid

1. **Shiny Object Syndrome** — Don't pivot the service, don't switch platforms, don't redesign the brand. Execute the plan for 90 days first.

2. **Perfectionism** — Ship the imperfect thing. A gig live beats a gig in drafts every time.

3. **Isolation** — Talk to people. Comment. DM. Follow up. The network IS the business.

|---|---|
