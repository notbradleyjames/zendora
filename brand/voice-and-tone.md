# Zendora — Voice & Tone Guide

> *How Zendora speaks — in proposals, captions, emails, and everywhere else.*

---

## 🎙️ The Core Voice

Zendora sounds like a **brilliant, trusted friend who happens to be an expert**.

Not corporate. Not stiff. Not trying too hard to be cool.  
Confident, warm, clear — with energy behind every word.

---

## 🧠 Voice Pillars

### 1. Confident (not arrogant)
We know our stuff, and we say so clearly — without putting others down or over-promising.

✅ **Do:** "We'll have your automation live within 48 hours."  
❌ **Don't:** "We're the best agency in the world and no one else can do this."

---

### 2. Direct (not blunt)
We cut to the chase. Clients are busy. Respect their time.

✅ **Do:** "Here's what we'll build, what it costs, and when it ships."  
❌ **Don't:** "We'd love to take the time to explore potentially aligning our synergistic capabilities..."

---

### 3. Human (not robotic)
Real people run this. Our writing should prove it.

✅ **Do:** "Honestly, this is one of my favorite types of projects."  
❌ **Don't:** "We acknowledge receipt of your inquiry and will respond accordingly."

---

### 4. Energetic (not exhausting)
We're excited — but we don't use 17 exclamation points per paragraph.

✅ **Do:** "This is going to be a game-changer for your workflow."  
❌ **Don't:** "WOW!! We are SO EXCITED!! This is AMAZING!!"

---

### 5. Smart (not academic)
We know the technical stuff. We translate it into plain language.

✅ **Do:** "We'll build a workflow that automatically routes new leads from your form into your CRM and sends them a welcome email."  
❌ **Don't:** "We'll implement a webhook-triggered n8n pipeline that interfaces with your Salesforce REST API endpoints."

---

## 📟 Tone by Context

| Context | Tone Adjustment |
|---|---|
| Proposal / Pitch | Professional + Enthusiastic. Show expertise. |
| Client Email | Warm + Direct. Clear next steps. |
| Social Media | Casual + Insightful. Add personality. |
| Error / Problem | Honest + Calming. Own it, fix it. |
| Invoice / Contract | Clear + No-nonsense. Just facts. |
| Onboarding | Welcoming + Reassuring. They made the right call. |

---

## 🚫 Words & Phrases to Avoid

| Avoid | Use Instead |
|---|---|
| "Synergy" | "Working together" / "combining both" |
| "Leverage" | "Use" / "take advantage of" |
| "Circle back" | "Follow up" / "check in" |
| "Deliverables" (overused) | "What we'll build" / "what you'll get" |
| "World-class" | Show it, don't say it |
| "Innovative" | Describe the actual innovation |
| "ASAP" | Give a specific time/date |

---

## ✍️ Writing Patterns We Love

**Short punchy sentences.** Followed by a little context when needed.

Use **bold** to highlight the exact thing someone should notice.

> Blockquotes for client quotes, key insights, or memorable lines.

Lists when there's more than two things to say:
- First point
- Second point  
- Third point

---

## 📱 Social Media Voice (Specific)

For platforms like LinkedIn, Instagram, Twitter/X:

- **Lead with the insight**, not the fluff
- **Tell stories** — before/after, lessons learned, client wins
- **Ask one question** at the end to drive engagement
- **Short paragraphs** — max 3 lines before a line break
- **Emojis**: 1–3 per post max. Used for structure, not decoration.

**Example Post (LinkedIn):**

> Most founders waste 10+ hours a week on tasks that could be automated.
>
> Not because they don't know it's a problem.
> Because they don't have time to fix it.
>
> That's exactly what we fix at Zendora.
>
> What's one task in your business you wish just... happened automatically?
