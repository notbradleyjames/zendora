## 📋 Discovery Questions

1. Do you have any existing brand assets? (logos, colors, fonts)
2. Who is your target audience? Describe them.
3. What 3 words should your brand feel like?
4. Share 3–5 brands/designs you love and tell me why.
5. What platforms will this design live on?
6. Do you need editable files or just final exports?
7. Is there a launch date or event driving the timeline?

## 🗃️ Delivery Process

```
1. Discovery + Mood Board (24 hrs)
   └── Collect brand questionnaire answers
   └── Build a Pinterest/Figma mood board to align vision

2. Concept Phase (2–4 days)
   └── Deliver 2–3 initial directions
   └── Client picks one path

3. Refinement (1–2 rounds)
   └── Up to 2 rounds of revisions included
   └── Additional rounds at $75/round

4. Final Delivery
   └── Exported files: PNG, SVG, PDF, Figma link
   └── Brand kit PDF
   └── Canva/Figma templates handed off
```
