## 📋 Discovery Questions

1. What are you selling and who is it for?
2. What's your current monthly revenue / marketing budget?
3. What channels have you tried? What worked? What didn't?
4. What does a conversion look like for you? (call booked, purchase, signup?)
5. Who is your top competitor? What do they do well?
6. Do you have any customer testimonials or case studies?
7. What's the #1 thing holding your marketing back right now?

## 📐 Marketing Frameworks We Use

### The 1-3-1 Messaging Framework
- **1** Core problem your customer has
- **3** Ways you solve it
- **1** Outcome/result they can expect

### The AIDA Funnel
- **A**wareness → Content, Ads, SEO
- **I**nterest → Landing pages, email, retargeting
- **D**esire → Case studies, testimonials, demos
- **A**ction → CTA, offer, urgency

### The StoryBrand Model (simplified)
- Character (Customer) has a Problem
- Meets a Guide (You/Zendora)
- Guide gives them a Plan
- They take Action and achieve Success
- Failure is avoided
