## 1. Brand Questionnaire (Client Intake)

Use this for any new design or branding project.

```
ZENDORA — BRAND DISCOVERY QUESTIONNAIRE

Client Name:
Business Name:
Date:

--- ABOUT THE BUSINESS ---
1. In one sentence, what does your business do?

2. Who are your top 3 competitors? (Include URLs)

3. What makes you different from them?

4. What's your #1 goal for this brand project?
   [ ] Launch a new brand from scratch
   [ ] Rebrand / refresh existing identity
   [ ] Create assets for a specific campaign
   [ ] Other: ____________

--- AUDIENCE ---
5. Describe your ideal customer in 3 sentences.
   (Who are they? What do they want? What keeps them up at night?)

6. How old are they? ________ Gender: ________ Income: ________

7. What 3 words would they use to describe a brand they love?

--- BRAND PERSONALITY ---
8. If your brand were a person, who would they be? (Celebrity, character, archetype)

9. Circle 3 words that describe your brand:
   Bold / Playful / Minimal / Luxurious / Warm / Technical / Trustworthy / 
   Edgy / Professional / Friendly / Energetic / Calm / Modern / Classic

10. Share 3–5 brands (in any industry) that you love the look/feel of.
    Tell me WHY you like each one.

11. Share 1–2 brands that you absolutely do NOT want to look like.
    What don't you like about them?

--- VISUAL PREFERENCES ---
12. Color preferences? Any colors you love or hate?

13. Are there any existing brand colors/fonts you want to keep?

14. Describe the visual feeling you want: (e.g., "Clean + modern like Apple" 
    or "Warm and earthy like a farmers market")

--- PRACTICAL DETAILS ---
15. Where will this brand be used? (Check all that apply)
    [ ] Website  [ ] Social Media  [ ] Print  [ ] Packaging  
    [ ] Presentations  [ ] Signage  [ ] Other: ________

16. What file formats do you need? (PNG / SVG / PDF / Figma access)

17. Do you need editable templates (Canva/Figma) handed off? [ ] Yes  [ ] No

18. Timeline: When do you need this by? ________________

19. Anything else I should know?
```

## 4. Email Sequence Framework

```
WELCOME SEQUENCE STRUCTURE

Email 1 — Deliver + Welcome (Day 0, immediately)
Purpose: Fulfill the promise that got them to sign up
Subject: "Here's what you asked for, [NAME]"
Content: Deliver lead magnet / welcome gift + what to expect next
CTA: None / light (just read)

Email 2 — Your Story (Day 2)
Purpose: Build connection + trust
Subject: "Why I started [BRAND/THING]"
Content: Origin story — the problem you faced, the journey, the turning point
CTA: Reply (engagement)

Email 3 — Your Best Content / Quick Win (Day 4)
Purpose: Deliver value, establish expertise
Subject: "[Number] ways to [achieve result]" or "The [framework] that changed everything"
Content: Actionable teaching / insight they can use immediately
CTA: Try this + save/bookmark

Email 4 — Social Proof (Day 6)
Purpose: Build confidence in your authority
Subject: "What happened when [CLIENT] tried this..."
Content: Case study / testimonial / before-after story
CTA: "Curious if this could work for you?" → link to discovery call or product

Email 5 — The Offer (Day 8)
Purpose: Convert
Subject: "Ready for [RESULT]? Here's how we can help."
Content: Introduce the paid offer, remind them of pain, show solution
CTA: Book a call / Buy / Learn more

---

OPTIONAL EXTENSIONS:
Email 6 (Day 12): FAQ / Objection handler
Email 7 (Day 16): Urgency / last chance
Email 8 (Day 20): "Break up" email (re-engagement)
```
